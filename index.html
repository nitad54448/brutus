      About Brutus

      This program uses a system-specific, exhaustive GPU search to find potential unit cells based on the first 20 peaks. The checked crystal systems are searched one after the other, highest symmetry first; rhombohedral (R) lattices are found by the hexagonal search and reported in hexagonal axes. WebGPU must be available and active in the browser; without it only cubic, tetragonal and hexagonal can be searched (on the CPU).

      All trial cells are refined, scored (M(20)), and analyzed for space groups. For a full breakdown, read the technical guide.

      Supported file formats

      Generic 2-column (2θ, intensity; a “Wavelength” header line is read), .xrdml, Bruker RawData .xml, .ras, .uxd, .udf, GSAS (.gsa, .esd, .std, .xra), FullProf .dat and pdCIF. Data, raw or Kα2-stripped, can be saved in several formats.

      Working with it

      1. Data: load a pattern, check the radiation and the 2θ range.
      2. Peaks: adjust the background and peak search until the peak list is clean; Ctrl+click on the plot adds a peak.
      3. Indexing: choose the crystal systems and the GPU search parameters, then press Index (Esc or the red button stops the search).
      4. Solutions: open a solution to see its cell, Niggli cell and space group; the Solution menu refines it, tests its space group, converts it to a sub- or supergroup, explores a space group by hand, or exports a pdCIF.

      Projects (File → Save project) are JSON files: pattern, peaks, solutions with their space groups, settings, run history and log.

      Refine MC

      Monte-Carlo search around the selected cell, constrained to its crystal system. Zero is refined as an extra parameter when "Refine Zero" is on. The result is added as a new solution only if it improves M20.

      Space Group MC

      Every space group of this crystal system is tried as a hypothesis: its forbidden reflections are removed from the calculated line list, the cell is re-refined against what is left, and the evidence is recomputed. Rule sets that produce the same calculated pattern for this cell, wavelength, range and tolerance cannot be separated by the data, so they share one row and are labelled by extinction symbol rather than by a representative group. Ranking is in two tiers. A row with a hard violation the impurity allowance does not cover is falsified - a single genuine reflection on a forbidden line rules a group out - and can never outrank a row with none, whatever its score. Falsified rows are shown below, ordered least-bad first. Score is the log-odds in nats against the leading row of its own tier: it rewards forbidden lines that are cleanly empty (Clean) and penalises observed peaks that only a forbidden line explains (Viol, hard count with soft Ka2/weak/ambiguous ones in brackets) and peaks the refined cell cannot explain at all (Unidx). Rows within about 2.3 nats of each other are a tie. M20 describes the refined cell only - it rises whenever lines are deleted, so it is not what ranks the hypotheses, so the table is sorted by Score by default. Click any header to sort by that column instead, and click it again to reverse. The row leading on Score stays bold wherever the sort puts it. Select a row to keep its refined cell.

      Explore space group
